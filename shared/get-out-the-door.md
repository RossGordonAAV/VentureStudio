# Get out the door

Ross rates talking to people above any desk work. A conversation is usually the fastest, cheapest test there is.

## Rule for every seat
- Before proposing any other test, ask: **could one conversation answer this faster?** If yes, that's the test.
- When a conversation is the answer, **do a quick search and name real people**, not "talk to some hospitals".

## Who to talk to first (Ross, 20 Nov 2026)
1. **UvA academics first** (and Amsterdam UMC where relevant): people Ross can easily buy a coffee to talk through the real problems. List these before anyone else.
2. Only then move up the value chain: policy experts, then industry, incumbents, buyers.

## How Ross introduces himself (Ross, 20 Nov 2026)
In every outreach draft: "I'm Ross Gordon, investment manager at Amsterdam Academic Ventures – we're exploring some concepts for one of our companies." Never introduce him as a chemical engineer, and don't describe the idea as his own side project.

## Quick people search (≤10 minutes per round)
Data-based first, so every name has a source:
- **Papers and patents** (PubMed, Google Scholar, Espacenet): authors on the key recent work – CSO's main source.
- **Company pages and press** (team pages, funding news): people at competitors, ex-competitors, likely customers.
- **LinkedIn search results** via web search: role + organisation type + region (Amsterdam / NL / EU first).
- **Event speaker lists and grant databases** (CORDIS, NWO, RVO): people already working on the problem.
- **Ross's network**: flag when someone sits near his world (UvA, VU, Amsterdam UMC, AAV portfolio, ACE) so a warm intro is possible. Read-only; never write to AAV bases.

Never invent a person, role or link. No source → don't list them.

## Output
Up to five people per round, best first, written as new rows in Airtable → **People to talk to** (Status `Suggested`):
Name · Organisation · Role · Type · **Question to ask** (one) · Why them · Source link · Route in · Suggested by (seat).

Ross ticks which to approve. Go-to-Market drafts the outreach note for approved ones; nothing is sent without his OK.

## Scoring
A round's proposed test gets a bonus in the CEO synthesis if it involves meeting someone this week. The audit team must say why if it prefers a desk-based alternative.
