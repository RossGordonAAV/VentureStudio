## Why the gap exists
**WHY INCUMBENTS AREN'T DOING THIS**
- Germany has had a 25 ct can deposit since 1 Jan 2003 with 98.5% return ([DUH](https://www.duh.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilung/20-jahre-dosenpfand-deutsche-umwelthilfe-sieht-pfandsysteme-als-erfolgsmodell-und-fordert-ausweit/)). 20+ years and the market settled on cheap, high-volume bins (€13–33) for mixed bottles and cans. **Known.** That suggests buyers want capacity and low price, not a design object. **Believed.**
- A 10-can tube holds €1.50 of deposit; bins hold ~200 cans. Low capacity per footprint makes it a niche SKU for a brand selling by the hundred thousand. **Believed.**
- Single-format product: tubes only take one can diameter/height (330/440/250 ml), while incumbents sell one bin for everything incl. PET. **Believed.**

**WHERE THE GAP APPEARS TO BE**
- Dutch households in small flats storing *cans only*, who care about drips/smell and keeping cans undented. Advice pieces say upright storage stops leaks and bags cause dents ([id.nl](https://id.nl/huis-en-entertainment/huishouden/schoonmaken/statiegeldblikjes-handig-en-hygienisch-bewaren-zo-doe-je-dat)). **Known.** Nobody sells an upright, sealed, carry-and-empty format for this. **Known (not found).**

**RISKS TO THE GAP**
- **The core pain is being designed out.** Verpact agreed (Nov 2025) that most machines will accept dented cans ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/11/27/extra-maatregelen-om-problemen-met-statiegeld-en-zwerfafval-aan-te-pakken); [Kassa, May 2026](https://www.bnnvara.nl/kassa/artikelen/miljoenen-euros-aan-statiegeld-blijven-liggen-consumenten-leveren-blikjes-en-flesjes-niet-of-te-beschadigd-in)), and bulk points go from 77 to 300. If a bag of dented cans works, "keep them uncrushed and feed 10 fast" loses value. **Known (policy); impact Believed.** Note: in Apr 2026 Verpact still said dented cans could not be taken ([Hart van Nederland](https://www.hartvannederland.nl/economie/geld/artikelen/noord-brabant-boxtel-blikjes-statiegeld-angelique-probleem)) – rollout status unclear.
- **Copy risk:** PVC tube + screw cap is not protectable; no-name Amazon sellers already make fridge can dispensers and could list a tube in weeks. **Believed.**
- Printables already hosts free can-dispenser files. **Known.**

## Why we win / why we lose
- **Win:** only upright, sealed, carry-and-empty format; small footprint; design-store fit. Can deposit only since 1 Apr 2023 ([Verpact](https://www.verpact.nl/en/node/452)), so the NL category is young.
- **Lose:** €13 bins and a free bin bag; dented-can acceptance and bulk return remove the pain; one-off purchase, no recurring revenue; trivial to copy.

## Why now
NL cans entered the deposit in April 2023 and the deposit rise was dropped ([DutchNews, Nov 2025](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2025/11/plans-to-hike-deposits-on-plastic-bottles-and-cans-shelved)) – so "why now" rests on a young habit, not a rising fee. **Known.**

## Pre-mortem (CB Insights)
1. **No market need** – dented-can acceptance and bulk points made a bag good enough.
2. **Get outcompeted** – Amazon/Bol copies at a third of the price.
3. **Need/lack business model** – one-off product, price not set [Ross: confirm], no repeat purchase, small NL-only market.

## Strategic test
Ask Verpact this week whether and when dented cans are accepted at most machines. If "yes, already", the brief's core pain claim must be rewritten before any WTP test.
