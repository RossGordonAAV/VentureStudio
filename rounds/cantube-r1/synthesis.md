# CEO synthesis – Statiegeld can tube – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

## We believe
- **Making it is easy.** Stock 75 mm pipe, an off-the-shelf screw cap and a 3D-printed base. 5–10 units can be built this week with no tooling. (Known)
- **Nobody sells a vertical can tube.** No NL or DE product was found. The category is already served by cheap bins: €13–18 for 95–100 litres, about 200 cans (Songmics, EasyMaxx/Lidl). Germany has had a deposit for 20+ years and settled on cheap, high-capacity bins. (Known)
- **Part of the pain is being designed out.**
  - Verpact agreed in Nov 2025 that most machines will accept dented cans. Rollout status is unclear.
  - Bulk return points are going from 77 to 300, and bulk machines get a bigger role from 2028.
  - Storage, smell and drips stay real; "keep them uncrushed and feed 10 fast" weakens. (Known policy; impact Believed)
- **Revenue is transactional.** One-off durable product. Add-ons give repeat sales, not recurring revenue. Horeca already returns cans free through wholesaler bags. (Known / Believed)
- **The market is big in principle.** 8.47m NL households and ~2.5bn cans a year. The EU packaging rules require a deposit in every member state by 2029. (Known)
- **Copying is trivial.** Only the look of the base can be protected (Benelux design registration, €150). (Known)
- **Shipping a 1.2–1.5 m tube is the cost that decides the margin.** (Believed)

## We don't know
- The price strangers will pay against the €13 bin anchor.
- The cost of shipping a 1.2 m parcel.
- Whether dented-can acceptance is live at most machines.

## Outcome type
- **Scalable company:** weak. Easy to copy, a low price anchor and no recurring revenue.
- **Side business:** possible. Low set-up cost, no tooling, and Marktplaats local pick-up avoids shipping. Hours rise with each unit made by hand.
- **Alternative wedge (B2B2C):** Verpact misses its 90% legal target (cans 83–86%) and has threatened-fine pressure. A Verpact- or supermarket-branded return aid would mean one buyer with a budget. (Guessed)

## Testability: Easy
A real signal (paid orders from strangers) can be had in 14 days for the cost of materials.

## Proposed decision: Keep going as a side-business test only
No tooling, no brand build, no retail approach until strangers pay.
