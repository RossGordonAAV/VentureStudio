# Audit – Statiegeld can tube – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: Fund with changes.** Cheap, quick and aimed at the right question (will strangers pay?). As written, though, a "pass" could still lose money and leave the bigger risk untested.

## Weakest points
1. **The side-business case can't be judged on this page.** Price, unit cost, set-up cost, hours per unit and margin are all [Ross: confirm]. Five orders at a price with no margin would count as a pass.
2. **The test checks whether people will pay. It doesn't check whether the problem lasts.** If machines accept dented cans and bulk return points spread, the "keep them uncrushed" reason weakens (impact only Believed). Feeding one tube into one machine proves little. "Nobody sells one" is equally consistent with "nobody needs one".
3. **Building 10 units first is more than the test needs, and the B2B2C wedge is Guessed.** A Verpact co-brand call costs Ross time on a buyer who is unlikely to engage with a hand-made product.

## Alternative actions
1. **Price-ladder pre-sale with one prototype (instead of building 10).**
   - Method: build one unit and photograph it. Post 2–3 Marktplaats listings in Amsterdam at different prices, taking paid reservations. Build only what is reserved.
   - Pass: ≥5 paid reservations from strangers at or above a minimum price that clears margin [Ross: confirm].
   - Kill: <2 reservations, or demand only below that minimum.
2. **Set the unit economics before listing.**
   - Method: cost one unit (materials, print time, Ross's build hours). Fix the minimum price and the hours-per-week ceiling.
   - Pass: margin per unit and hours/week fit Ross's side-business threshold [Ross: confirm].
   - Kill: margin can't clear that threshold at a plausible price premium over the €13 bin.
3. **Pain check at the machine (alongside, ~2 hours).**
   - Method: 10 short conversations with people returning cans at an AH or Jumbo machine or a bulk return point, asking how they store cans now and what annoys them.
   - Pass: ≥5/10 name space, smell or drips as a real pain the bin doesn't solve.
   - Kill: most are content with a bag or bin.
   - Park the Verpact call until the sales data exists.
