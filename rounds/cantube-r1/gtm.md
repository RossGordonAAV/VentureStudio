# CanTube R1 – Go-to-Market (20 Nov 2026)

## Testability verdict: **Easy**
- **Test:** sell 10 hand-made V2 tubes to strangers in 14 days through a one-product pre-order page (iDEAL payment, ship in 3 weeks, full refund promise) plus a Marktplaats listing, with a small Instagram/Facebook ad push in NL. Cost: tube material (Ross's sheet: ~€25–32 per 5 m length, 3–4 tubes) + ad budget **[Ross: confirm]**.
- **Real yes:** money paid (or a paid deposit) by someone outside friends/family. Sign-ups, likes and "I'd buy one" do not count. **Known** – pre-sales guidance: only payment counts; exclude friends-and-family buyers; set the threshold before launch ([Kromatic](https://kromatic.com/real-startup-book/2-evaluative-market-experiments/value-proposition-test/pre-sales-smoke-test/)).
- **What would mislead:** (1) friends/family orders; (2) too little traffic – zero sales from 100 visits means nothing; (3) deep launch discounts; (4) virality without sales – a students' statiegeld bin got ~2.7m views in 24 h, but nothing was sold ([IamExpat, Apr 2026](https://iamexpat.nl/lifestyle/lifestyle-news/bin-designed-amsterdam-students-separate-statiegeld-deposits-goes-viral)); (5) changing the price mid-test.

## Who buys, and the pain (evidence)
- **Known:** storing cans at home is a recognised consumer topic. Consumer site ID.nl says cans take up a lot of space, leak, smell, attract fruit flies, and dented cans can be rejected; its suggestions are bags, boxes or a can rack "from Amazon" – **no dedicated product or price named** ([ID.nl](https://id.nl/huis-en-entertainment/huishouden/schoonmaken/statiegeldblikjes-handig-en-hygienisch-bewaren-zo-doe-je-dat)).
- **Known:** machine rejections of damaged packs frustrate users (e.g. 2–3 of a 20-item bag rejected); Verpact says the share lost to rejection is "a small percentage" and machines now accept slight dents ([Kassa](https://www.bnnvara.nl/kassa/artikelen/miljoenen-euros-aan-statiegeld-blijven-liggen-consumenten-leveren-blikjes-en-flesjes-niet-of-te-beschadigd-in)).
- **Known:** existing dedicated statiegeld bins (60–200 L) are sold to businesses at ~€72.50–€215 ex VAT; no home use mentioned ([Binqer](https://www.binqer.nl/inzamelbakken-voor-statiegeldflesjes/bonton)). Home-design competitor: **not found**.
- **Reddit / consumer survey on home storage:** not found in this round.
- **Risk – Known:** from 1 Jan 2028 more stores must take back cans and bulk machines get a bigger role ([Rijksoverheid, Sep 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/09/04/kabinet-stelt-makkelijker-statiegeldsysteem-voor)). **Believed:** easier returns could cut storage time – or reward bulk storage. Ask buyers.
- **Beachhead (Believed):** apartment households in Dutch cities who drink canned drinks weekly and return by bike – small kitchens, no garage, carrying is the pain.

## Pricing hypothesis (structure only)
- Transactional: single tube (entry) + 3–4 tube hub bundle (anchor) + add-on tubes/250 ml sleeve (repeat). **No recurring model found** – add-ons are the only repeat angle (**Believed**). Run two price points on split pages; numbers **[Ross: confirm]** – no sourced home-product price exists.

## Demand test this week
Build page + Marktplaats listing Mon–Tue; ads Wed–Sun; second week repeat.
- **Pass:** ≥10 paid orders from strangers and ≥3% paid conversion of page visitors in 14 days (thresholds set now, per Kromatic).
- **Kill:** <1% paid conversion after ≥500 visitors, or 0 stranger sales on Marktplaats.
- **Pivot signal:** clicks high but checkout drop-off high → test the lower price / single tube.
