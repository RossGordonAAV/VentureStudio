# Go-to-Market – Science Park grid batteries – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Headline:** At Science Park the buyer is already acting without us. UvA has a named grid-congestion lead and has used a consultant plus a small battery to get LabQ built. The gap is less likely to be "nobody thought of a battery" and more likely to be "who owns, finances and operates it, and on which Liander contract". (Believed)

## Who owns, decides and pays
- The park is a joint initiative of UvA, the City of Amsterdam and NWO. No single park operator or parkmanagement body was found. **Known** ([UvA campus](https://campus.uva.nl/en/science-park/development-science-park/amsterdam-science-park-in-development.html))
- Matrix Innovation Centers is owned by the City of Amsterdam, UvA, VU and Rabobank. Its MD since 2019 is Ivo Sweep (probably Ross's "Evo"; please confirm). Its Science Park business has been split off from Zuidas, organisationally and financially. **Known** ([Consultancy.nl](https://www.consultancy.nl/nieuws/68658/strategische-financiering-en-herstructurering-voor-matrix-innovation-centers))
- UvA: Liander turned down further capacity requests and UvA is on the waiting list. Energy is run through the UvA/HvA Facility Services energy programme. Rowan Boeters is portfolio manager for energy transition and grid congestion. **Known** ([UvA news 2024](https://www.uva.nl/shared-content/uva/en/news/news/2024/07/impact-of-overloaded-electricity-grid-on-uvas-new-construction-and-sustainability.html))
- Precedent: LabQ needed about 700 kW. Haskoning found about 600 kW of savings across the Faculty of Science buildings, and a small battery covers the gap until 2027. **Known** ([Haskoning](https://www.haskoning.com/en/projects/uva-establishes-new-lab-through-clever-grid-congestion-strategy))
- **Buyer vs user:** for Matrix, the buyer is the Matrix MD and the budget sits in Matrix's real-estate capex and financing. The users are the tenants, who want space they can occupy. For UvA, the buyer is Facility Services and the energy programme. (Believed)

## Beachhead and wider market
- **Beachhead:** Matrix's next Science Park building. Trigger: a building that cannot get its connection, or has to be built semi-passive. (Believed)
- **Scale-up:** Noord-Holland business parks with a collective body (energy cooperative or parkmanagement):
  - Boekelermeer, Alkmaar: Liander, the municipality and a cooperative still being set up run a 5 MWh battery plus group contract. **Known** ([NH Nieuws](https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/348635/start-energiehub-alkmaar-slim-stroomnet-met-een-megabatterij))
  - Hoorn80: parkmanagement via ECWF, about 200 firms. No battery yet. **Known** ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/duurzame-bedrijventerreinen/hoorn80))
  - Baanstee Noord, Purmerend: energy hub on the way. **Known** ([Rodi](https://www.rodi.nl/regiozakenpurmerend/bedrijventerreinen/488711/energiehub-op-komst-in-baanstee-noord))
- About 560 Amsterdam customers are still on Liander's waiting list. Flexible contracts and behind-the-meter fixes are starting in Amsterdam-Noord, with West to follow. **Known** ([Solar Magazine](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i42118/liander-vindt-ruimte-op-stroomnet-voor-138-amsterdamse-bedrijven))

## Rules that shape the offer (GTM view only)
- **Liander FlexPlus Batterij:** half of the battery serves the site and half is available to Liander for up to 4 hours a day. The company gets a guaranteed monthly fee, but no waiting-list priority. Flex-E can cover up to 40% of the investment. **Known** ([Solar Magazine](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i44673/bedrijven-kunnen-wachtlijst-omzeilen-door-batterij-te-delen-met-stroomnet))
- **Group transport agreements (GTOs):** grid operators must offer them from 1 Jan 2027. Netbeheer Nederland says they are "no simple escape route" from the waiting list. **Known** ([Solar Magazine](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i43328/groepstransportovereenkomst-geen-simpele-ontsnappingsroute-voor-bedrijven-op-de-wachtlijst))

## Pricing hypothesis
- **Battery-as-a-service:** Boltainer rents batteries monthly with no upfront investment, on 5-, 7- or 9-year terms, with an option to buy. It reports 10–15 congestion enquiries a day. No prices were published. **Known** ([Solar Magazine](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i42730/boltainer-start-met-verhuren-van-batterijen))
- **Shared-savings battery offers in NL:** not found.
- **To test:** a monthly fee per kW of capacity unlocked, stacked with the FlexPlus fee and market trading. The value anchor is the cost of not building, or of building semi-passive. The price is not set yet. [Ross: confirm figures]
