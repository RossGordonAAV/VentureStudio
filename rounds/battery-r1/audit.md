# Audit – Science Park grid batteries – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: Fund with changes.** The reframe is sound. But the test checks whether one site has a problem. It doesn't check whether anyone would pay for the capacity desk, or whether the desk would be more than a consultancy job.

## Weakest points
1. **No buyer or price on the page (Believed gap).** UvA has already solved LabQ with Haskoning. If Matrix can hire the same consultancy, the desk is a service line, not a company. The test never asks the customer to pay.
2. **The pass/kill criteria are lopsided.** The only kill is "TenneT-blocked", and right-sizing a request may still be worth doing there. If Matrix has no building with a kW gap, or won't share meter data, nothing is killed, so a weak result would probably come back as "keep going".
3. **The fallback is a different idea.** "Kill → Liander-only industrial park" swaps the customer and the load profile (peaky, not flat). That's a new round, not a pivot of this one. Also, "Evo" is still unconfirmed for a warm lead.

## Alternative actions
1. **Ask for money inside the Matrix conversation.** Method: offer a priced pilot to measure and right-size one blocked building (fee [Ross: confirm]). Pass: Matrix agrees in writing to a paid or co-funded pilot. Kill: they'd do it in-house or through their existing consultant.
2. **Repeatability check (conversations).** Method: speak to two more lab-building owners or campus estates, e.g. Utrecht Science Park estates (already named in the synthesis). Pass: at least two of three have a request sized on design figures with no measured load data. Kill: they already measure, or already use a consultancy for this.
3. **Desk check before calling Liander (about 1 hour).** This is desk-based because the question has a yes/no answer and is believed to be in public congestion data, so it shouldn't use up the Liander contact. Method: check the published congestion status for the Science Park substation. Pass: only Liander is constrained. Kill: TenneT-constrained, so stop the Science Park round. The Liander call can then focus on FlexPlus or a group contract.
