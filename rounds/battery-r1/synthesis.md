# CEO synthesis – Science Park grid batteries – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

## We believe
- **A battery on its own doesn't create capacity.** Behind the meter, it keeps a site within its contracted capacity. It only unlocks more when it sits inside a Liander contract: FlexPlus Batterij (Sep 2026), a capacity steering contract, or a group transport agreement (offered by law from Jan 2027). (Known)
- **TenneT may be the real wall.** 371 of 449 waitlisted Amsterdam applications are also blocked by TenneT, and flexible contracts aren't possible there. Which layer blocks Science Park is not known, and it decides everything. (Known / not found)
- **The obvious products already exist.**
  - Liander FlexPlus: the battery is sized at about twice the need, half is reserved for Liander, Liander pays a monthly fee, and there is no waiting-list priority.
  - Flex-E subsidy covers 40% of the cost.
  - Players: Boltainer rents batteries (10–15 congestion enquiries a day), Kenter Groendus sells hubs as a service, Energeion runs a 20 MW park battery, plus Scholt and GIGA Storage. (Known)
- **UvA has already solved one case.** For LabQ (about 700 kW), Haskoning measured actual loads and found about 600 kW of savings, so only a small battery was needed. Lab loads are flat, so there is little peak to shave. (Known; flat load from a US model, Believed for NL)
- **The scarce role is the park-level coordinator, not the battery.** There are about 480 hub initiatives but only 8 group contracts signed nationally (May 2026). The named bottlenecks are custom work, too little support capacity and weak business cases. (Known)
- **The money only works on avoided delay.** Trading alone doesn't pay. A battery pays where the cost of not building, or of building semi-passive, exceeds the capex. (Known, CE Delft)

## We don't know
- Whether Science Park is blocked at Liander or at TenneT.
- What a year of delay, or a semi-passive redesign, costs Matrix per kW it can't get.
- The FlexPlus fee and NL turnkey battery capex.
- Whether "Evo" is Ivo Sweep (MD of Matrix since 2019).

## Proposed decision: Keep going, reframed as a quick win
Don't sell a battery. Sell a **capacity desk** for new lab buildings:
- measure the real load;
- right-size the connection request;
- choose between FlexPlus, time-bound capacity, a capacity-limiting contract, a shared battery and load steering;
- file the application.

The battery becomes one tool among several. This scales to other campuses (Utrecht Science Park expects gensets and batteries until about 2031) and to congested industrial parks with peakier loads.

Wedge verdict: **too close to incumbents** for a park battery or hub. The sharper wedge is lab buildings whose requests are sized on design figures. The risk is that this is Haskoning-style consultancy, a feature rather than a company, unless the lab-load data feeds a product that can be repeated.
