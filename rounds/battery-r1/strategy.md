# Strategy (CSO) – Science Park grid batteries – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Headline:** The market is crowded and the rules now exist. The open question is local. In Amsterdam, 371 of 449 waitlisted large-connection requests are also blocked by TenneT's high-voltage grid, and flexible contracts are not possible there (Known – [Solar Magazine, 2 Oct 2026](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i44957/eindelijk-ruimte-op-het-amsterdamse-stroomnet-305-ondernemers-hebben-aansluiting-alsnog-binnen)). If Science Park is one of those, a battery is unlikely to unlock new capacity there (Believed).

## Why we win / why we lose
- **Win (Believed):** The scarce role is the coordinator, not the battery. Hubs are custom work, support capacity is short, and business cases look unattractive. Small and large users cannot yet be combined in one GTO. About 480 hub initiatives exist, but only 8 GTOs had been signed nationally by May 2026 (Known – [Versnellingsagenda Energiehubs](https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-08/Versnellingsagenda-Energiehubs.pdf)). A park-level orchestrator using GTO + battery + FlexPlus has room.
- **Lose (Known/Believed):** Liander sells the battery-for-capacity deal directly, and rental/BaaS firms already serve firms one by one. The TenneT constraint blocks most Amsterdam requests. For many firms energy is not a top-10 cost and grid products feel too complex (Known – [BlueTerra/Trinomics for EZK, Jul 2026](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i44180/onderzoek-congestiemanagement-bij-bedrijven-schiet-tekort)). Behind-the-meter batteries can also worsen congestion if traded on markets, and their effect must be proven case by case with the grid operator (Known – [CE Delft for Amsterdam](https://openresearch.amsterdam/image/2025/4/9/energyhubs_en_batterijopslag.pdf)).

## Why now
The toolkit arrived in 2024–26, according to the [ACM overview, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acm.nl/system/files/documents/overzicht-netcongestiemaatregelen-update-11-november-2026.pdf) (Known):
- non-firm ATO (2024–25)
- time-block rights (Apr 2025)
- capacity-steering contracts and the prioritisation framework (Dec 2025)
- GTO (in force Jan 2026; must be offered from 1 Jan 2027)
- Liander FlexPlus Batterij (Sept 2026)
- RVO Flex-E subsidy, up to 40% of the battery cost (Sept 2026)

Time-dependent tariffs for large distribution connections are due late 2026 or early 2027.

## Pre-mortem (CB Insights)
1. **No market need (primary):** Science Park sits behind the TenneT constraint, so a battery only shaves peaks within the existing connection. It wins no new capacity (Guessed until checked).
2. **Get outcompeted:** Liander, rental/BaaS firms and installers already cover single-site deals (Known).
3. **Need/lack business model:** Coordination is slow and custom, and the effort-to-reward ratio is poor for tenants (Known – EZK study).

Stalled examples: no named failed project found. Dutch grid-scale storage lags its neighbours on grid tariffs and tax: under 0.5 GW against Germany's 2.8 GW at end 2025 (Known – [IO+](https://ioplus.nl/en/posts/high-grid-costs-put-the-brakes-on-battery-powered-innovation)).

## Strategic test (this week)
One question decides the Science Park start: **is the Science Park waitlist Liander-only, or TenneT-constrained too, and would Liander accept FlexPlus Batterij or a GTO there?** If TenneT-constrained, the Science Park start should be parked. Industrial sites in Liander-only congestion would then be tested instead.
