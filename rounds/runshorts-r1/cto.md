# CTO – Leak-proof running shorts – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Core technical risk:** a thin running short that stays dry-feeling and chafe-free holds roughly 5–35 ml, but a stress-incontinence leak on a long run can be several times that. The gap is capacity per mm of thickness, not the barrier technology.

**What exists (Known unless marked)**
- Construction is commodity: wicking top layer → absorbent core → PU/TPU barrier → outer. A Chinese OEM sells exactly this for sport, at 15/25/35 ml ([LJ Vogues](https://www.ljvogues.com/sport-period-underwear.html)).
- Running shorts with built-in briefs already sold: Modibodi Running Short "up to 15 ml" with a merino top layer ([Modibodi](https://us.modibodi.com/products/running-short-moderate-heavy-black)); Knix Pursuit "about 1 regular tampon" and rated Light – Knix puts bladder leaks in its Heavy tier ([Knix](https://www.knix.ca/products/pursuit-leakproof-training-short)). Nike, Sweaty Betty, Hanes and Lumana also list leak-protection shorts (search results, specs not checked).
- Underwear goes much higher: Modibodi Ultra "up to 250 ml" ([Modibodi guide](https://modibodi.com/pages/absorbency-guide?region=au)) – Believed too bulky for a run.
- **Leak size:** in a 38-minute treadmill test, runners with running-induced SUI had median pad gain 24.3 g (range 3.9–166.3 g); continent runners 4.2 g, so part is sweat ([ICS 2024](https://www.ics.org/2024/abstract/208)). Over 2–3 hours: Believed several times that. **So current running shorts (≤15 ml) fall short for most of this group.**
- **Bowel leaks:** no textile product found claiming to contain them (not found). What exists: anal insert (Renew), physician-fitted vaginal balloon (Eclipse) ([NAFC](https://nafc.org/bhealth-blog/the-growing-array-of-options-for-managing-fecal-incontinence/)); prevention – low fibre the day before, nothing 2 h before, care with gels ([Mayo Clinic](https://sportsmedicine.mayoclinic.org/news/how-can-i-prevent-runners-diarrhea)); route planning. Recommend: urine only.
- **Devices that stop the leak instead:** Contiform pessary, NHS prescription or direct purchase, 3 sizes ([StressNoMore](https://www.stressnomore.co.uk/blog/contiform-vaginal-pessary-available-nhs-and-stressnomore)); Uresta, 97% of 40 runners/CrossFit users had fewer leaks, 90% still using at 12 months, $139 ([Running Magazine](https://runningmagazine.ca/sections/gear/stress-incontinence-in-women-runners-uresta-can-help)). These are the real competitor for "don't stop on a long run".
- **PFAS:** Thinx settled for $4m (+ up to $1m), no admission, committed to no intentionally added PFAS ([NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/consumer/thinx-settlement-period-underwear-details-rcna66743)). Specify a PFAS-free barrier and demand a lab report; supplier "PFAS-free" claims seen were unverified.
- **Chafing/breathability over 2–3 h:** no reviewer data found. Believed: wet core against skin plus gusset seams is the failure point; merino top layer and bonded (not sewn) edges are the known mitigations.
- **Classification:** disposable incontinence briefs are sold under MDR Class I (Hartmann MoliCare DoC title, [Hartmann](https://hartmann.info/en-ae/products/incontinence-management/all-in-one-incontinence-briefs/moderate-incontinence/molicare-premium-elastic)). Period pants are sold as clothing. Believed: an "incontinence" claim moves a garment towards MDR; "bladder leaks/sweat" wording is how incumbents stay apparel. [Regulatory adviser: confirm].
- **MOQs:** China OEM 50 pcs/style/colour, samples 72 h, bulk 30–45 days ([LJ Vogues](https://www.ljvogues.com/sport-period-underwear.html)); EU typical 500–2,000 per style/size, Portugal a cluster ([Wonnda](https://wonnda.com/product-types/menstrual-pants-manufacturers/)); Impetus (Portugal) makes 3-tampon briefs, OEM not stated ([Texfash](https://texfash.com/release/impetus-eco-cycle-the-first-collection-of-menstrual-briefs-from-the-portuguese-brand)); Confitex (NZ) does patented private label ([Nonwovens Industry](https://www.nonwovens-industry.com/contents/view_online-exclusives/2024-02-27/the-future-of-reusable-hygiene)). Turkey: not found. Unit prices: not found [Ross: confirm via quotes].

**Fake it first:** buy Modibodi/Knix shorts and a running pad; the four women wear-test on a ≥90-min run, weighing garment/pad before and after (same method as the ICS pad test), and score chafe and leak-through. Answers the showstopper – capacity needed vs held – in a week.

**IP note:** layer stacks are commodity; Confitex holds patents – check FTO only if Route C. Nothing worth patenting in Route A beyond design.
