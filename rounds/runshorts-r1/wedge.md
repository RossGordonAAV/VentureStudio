# Wedge check – Leak-proof running shorts – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Wedge verdict: Wedge too close to incumbents.** It is only a wedge if it is narrowed to *long-run capacity (and bowel backup)*.

**The wedge (as briefed):** women aged 40+ who have had children and run long distances, with leaks above light level. The incumbents already sell leak-proof running shorts to these women. Knix LeakStrong shorts and leggings hold up to 30 ml, launched Aug 2022 (Known, [Knix PR](https://www.retaildive.com/press-release/20220815-knix-announces-the-solution-to-leaks-with-all-new-leakstrongtm-activewear-1/)). Modibodi sells a running short, also 30 ml (Known, [Modibodi](https://www.modibodi.com/products/running-short-moderate-heavy-black)). Essity bought Knix (80%) and Modibodi in 2022 and calls leak-proof apparel the fastest-growing part of intimate hygiene (Known, [Essity](https://www.essity.com/media/press-release/essity-finalizes-acquisitions-of-knix-and-modibodi/e32202e66f8aeebf)). Small brands such as Prickly Pear Sports (US, founder-led) already target bladder leaks in sport (Known, [PPS](https://saffron-banjo-bkze.squarespace.com/aboutus)). Nike's leak short covers periods only (Known, [Nike](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-leak-protection-period-innovation)). "Light bladder leaks while running" is therefore Essity's core.

**What may be underserved:** capacity beyond about 30 ml on runs of 2 hours or more, chafing and heat over that time, and bowel urgency. 74% of female marathon runners reported an urge to defecate (Known, [Belfast study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1478552)). No product for bowel leaks while running was found. UI was found in 80% of athletes who had given birth, against 33% of those who had not (Known, small sample, [PMC8197179](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8197179/)). Actual leak volumes on long runs: not found (showstopper).

**Expansion path**
- Wedge → long-run/marathon women 40+ (capacity and anti-chafe) → ring 2: menopause, plus other high-impact sports such as trampolining, CrossFit and tennis (Believed). Knix already markets to menopause (Known, [Hello](https://hellomagazine.com/shopping/731389/knix-leakproof-briefs-for-menopause)).
- Ring 3: men after prostate surgery, sport-specific. TENA covers this only with underwear for daily use (Known, [TENA](https://shop.tena.co.uk/collections/washable-incontinence-pants-for-exercise)) (Guessed).
- Recurring revenue: a subscription for replacement liners or pairs (pads wear out with washing) is Guessed. A channel through pelvic-floor physios is Believed. Winning gives a running-club community and trust, not IP. That leverage is weak against Essity.
- Side business: private-label MOQ of 50–150 units is possible, but the thin liner then holds only about 8–15 ml (Known, [Ljvogues](https://www.ljvogues.com/case-study-leak-proof-running-shorts-skirts-activewear-startup.html)). Set-up cost and margin: [Ross: confirm].

## Why the gap exists
**WHY INCUMBENTS AREN'T DOING THIS:** a thin liner limits capacity. Above 2 mm the short gets hot, bulky and chafes (Known, Ljvogues). Incumbents design for the mass market (periods and light leaks), not for 2–4 hour runs (Believed). Bowel containment is a stigmatised, medical-feeling category that apparel brands avoid (Guessed).
**WHERE THE GAP APPEARS TO BE:** higher-capacity, anti-chafe shorts for long runs, possibly with a replaceable pad insert.
**RISKS TO THE GAP:** the need may be physics, not design: no textile may handle runner volumes. Essity can follow quickly. Physio treatment may cure the need (Contin-RUN tested pelvic-floor training, [NCT06595732](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06595732)).

**Question to answer next:** how much do these women actually leak on a long run, and does 30 ml fail? **Cheapest test:** the four women each run a long run in a Knix or Modibodi short, weigh it before and after, and note chafing. This costs a few pairs: [Ross: confirm budget].
