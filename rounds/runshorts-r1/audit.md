# Audit – Leak-proof running shorts – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: Fund with changes.** Cheap and quick, but the week-1 test checks a comfortable assumption on a convenience sample, and demand is only tested if that passes.

## Weakest points
1. **The case rests on long-run leak volume, which is only Believed.** The 24 g median is from 38 minutes. If most leakers lose well under 30 ml over 15–30 km, the gap disappears.
2. **The sample cannot carry the decision.** Four friends, not screened for leaking, in shorts they didn't choose. "2 of 4" is noise. Strangers who already leak and already have a workaround (pad, Uresta, physio) are the real market, and they aren't in week 1.
3. **Demand signals point the other way and the economics are unresolved.** Essity clearing both shorts at 50% suggests weak demand, not an open gap. £25–33 against $30–70 acquisition cost only works with a channel that isn't yet tested. Deposits of 5–19 have no defined outcome.

## Alternative actions
1. **Run the stranger pre-order now, in parallel, not after week 1.** Method: one page ("holds a long run, no chafing, swappable pad"), refundable deposit, posted in 2–3 NL women's running groups. Pass: ≥20 deposits from strangers. Kill: <10. 10–19 = one more channel, then decide. Price and deposit: [Ross: confirm].
2. **Recruit the wear test from leakers, not friends.** Method: screen 6–8 women in running groups who leak on runs ≥15 km; each logs one long run in her current set-up (weigh pad/garment), plus a 20-minute interview on workaround, spend and why she has or hasn't tried Uresta. Pass: ≥half report leak-through or chafing their current set-up doesn't solve. Kill: most are satisfied with pad or insert.
3. **Test the channel with three pelvic-floor physios before any product work.** Method: 20-minute calls (start with Heidi Moossdorff-Steinhauser). Ask what they recommend runners now and whether they would point patients to a high-capacity short. Pass: 2 of 3 would recommend or share it. Kill: all send runners to training or an insert. Also ask which claims keep it out of MDR.

**Opportunity cost:** tolerable as a side-business test if capped at a few hours a week; stop if both 1 and 2 miss.
