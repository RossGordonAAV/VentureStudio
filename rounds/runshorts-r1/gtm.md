# Go-to-Market – Leak-proof running shorts – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Testability: Easy.** A refundable pre-order (deposit via a Shopify/Stripe page, no stock) can be shown to strangers in women's running groups within 2 weeks at near-zero cost. Real signal = stranger deposits, not likes. *Believed.*

**Beachhead and buyer.** Mothers aged 40+ in NL training for a half or full marathon, whose leaks start past ~10 km. User = buyer = budget holder; trigger moment = signing up for a long race or a bad long run. *Believed.*

**Is the gap real? (feeds the showstopper)**
- Leak-proof running shorts already exist: Modibodi Running Short (30 ml, "moderate", marketed for periods/"leaks", "Run without limits or leaks") – [source](https://www.modibodi.com/products/running-short-moderate-heavy-black); Knix LeakStrong (~2.5 super tampons, "moderate" tier = cough/sneeze leaks; bladder leaks sit in heavier tiers) – [source](https://knix.ca/products/leakstrong-leakproof-short); Lumana leakproof running shorts sold in US mass retail – [source](https://www.walmart.com/ip/542039773); Sweaty Betty "Power 6" Leak Protect Shorts" (listing only; specs not retrieved) – [source](https://www.sweatybetty.com/eu/sale/sale-bottoms/sale-shorts-skorts/power-6"-leak-protect-shorts--SB10278_Black.html). *Known.*
- Messaging is period-first; bladder is an add-on. Capacity is pitched in tampons, not ml of urine. *Known (pages above).*
- Runner complaints: pads "leaving me raw from chafing" on longer runs; period pants "get very heavy", Modibodi plus pads "hasn't solved it completely" – [Mumsnet](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/exercise/4898320-any-other-incontinent-runners-out-there-chafing-issues). *Known (anecdote, UK).* So the angle is **capacity + chafing over 2–4 hours**, not "leak-proof shorts" as such.

**Language and stigma (NL).** NL retail says *urineverlies* (Kruidvat, Always Discreet) – [source](https://www.kruidvat.nl/campagne/urineverlies); NOC*NSF says *bekkenbodemklachten*, notes shame and "Doorbreek samen het taboe!", 25–45% of female athletes report pelvic floor complaints – [factsheet](https://cdn.nocnsf.nl/media/sgrhp1jh/factsheet-vrouwelijke-sporter-bekkenbodemgezondheid.pdf). Avoid *incontinentie* in headlines (reads as elderly care). *Believed.* Bowel leaks: lead with bladder only. *Believed.*
