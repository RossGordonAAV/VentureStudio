## Root cause (wrong caps)
The order was placed on the product title "Ø75". Nobody checked the cap's inner diameter against the pipe's 75 mm OD. The two shops disagree on what the 75 means (Gamma: connection size, slides over; Hornbach: outer diameter), and none gives the inner size. The rule that let it through: a nominal size was taken as a mating dimension.

## Measure first
1. Measure the inside of the V1 red cap (rim) and an ordered cap with a calliper, and note what goes wrong when the ordered cap is pushed on a V2 pipe end (too tight, too loose, or the wrong type).
2. Measure the outer diameter of the V1 red cap over the pipe, and the board thickness, before choosing a hole saw.
3. Stack 10 of the actual cans and measure the height (must be ≤ about 1,150 mm, with the cap fitted).

## Feedback to agents
- **Design lead:** add a critical-dimension row for cap ID ≥ 75.0 mm (soft push-on) and for hole = measured cap OD + 1–2 mm.
- **V1:** record the EAN and measured ID/OD of the working red cap. This is the reference part.
- **V2:** hold the speciedeksel order. If the measurement fails, test 1 × PPC afsluitkap 75 (B337004) on a pipe end in store before buying 3.
- **V3:** no 82–90 mm saw between sizes. Use 90 mm plus a felt or rubber shim, or size the hole from the measured cap OD.
