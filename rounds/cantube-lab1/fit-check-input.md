# Fit check input – Statiegeld can tube V2 (22 Nov 2026)

## What it must do
3 tubes, each storing 10 × 330 ml cans (66 mm Ø × 115 mm) stacked vertically. Cans dropped in the top must slide down freely (the slight air-cushion/vibration in a tight bore is wanted). Bottom closed with a removable soft cap (not glued). Tubes stand in a wooden base with holes. Home use only.

## Ross's existing V1 (works)
1 m grey Martens Ecomar Ø75 mm pipe (EAN 8711422075232) with a red push-on cap on the bottom.

## Cut and build plan
- Cut the 4 m pipe into 3 × 1,170 mm.
- Wrap each tube in one foil sheet; aluminium tape band round the top 3–5 cm.
- Red cap pushed over the bottom of each tube.
- Base: three 90 mm holes, centres ~100 mm apart in a triangle, capped tube ends drop into the holes.
