# V3 – volume – Statiegeld can tube – Lab 1 (21 Nov 2026)

**V3 in one sentence:** a flat-packed 3-tube cluster made from stock, ready-coloured 75 mm tube cut in half and joined with a stock sleeve, so the parcel stays under ~95 cm, with only the base/collar and screw cap ever moulded. **Improves over V2:** look (factory colour, no painted drainpipe), cost (no custom extrusion), shipping (avoids long-parcel surcharges). *Believed.*

## Shipping (the believed margin killer)
- PostNL consumer: up to 10 kg max 100 × 50 × 50 cm, €7.45 home; longer parcels only in the 23 kg band (max 176 × 78 × 58 cm), €17.45 (Known, [PostNL tarievenboekje juli 2026](https://www.postnl.nl/api/assets/blt43aa441bfc1e29f2/blt5e65605bca8690e0/tarievenboekje-12-juli-2026.pdf)).
- Business via MyParcel: PostNL base €6.25 excl. VAT at 0–249 parcels/month, €5.65 at 1,000–2,499 (Known, [MyParcel](https://www.myparcel.nl/en/services/sending-a-parcel-within-the-netherlands/?sf_paged=2)); PostNL over 95 × 70 × 58 cm +€4.85; DHL 120–180 cm +€7.25 NL / +€23.50 BE; PostNL EU long DE +€11.00 (Known, [MyParcel carrier specs 2026](https://www.myparcel.nl/app/uploads/myparcel-vervoerdersspecificaties-2026-nl.pdf)).
- So a full-length 1,170 mm box adds ~€4.85 per NL parcel; a 1,520 mm box also exceeds DHL's 120 cm. A split tube (2 × ~585 mm or 760 mm + sleeve) fits under 95 cm and removes the surcharge. *Calculation from Known figures; volume surcharge (> 50 dm³) still to check per box size.*

## Design changes for volume
- Split each tube in two with a stock sleeve; butt joint inside the sleeve keeps the bore smooth. *Believed – test can descent at the joint.*
- Use factory-coloured stock tube (Marley anthracite; or powder-coated aluminium) – no painting. *Bore of Marley pipe not published → [Ross: measure].*
- One moulded base + one collar holds 3 tubes; snap-fit, no screws, no glue.
- Keep the stock screw cap for V3a; mould an own-colour cap only once volume justifies it.
- Flat-pack: tubes nested side by side, base/collar in the same box.

## Compliance
- **GPSR** (in force 13 Dec 2024): risk assessment (tip-over, wall bracket), technical file, records ≥10 years, name/address/product identifier on product, same info plus picture and warnings on Marktplaats/web listings, accident reporting (Known, [CMS](https://cms.law/en/che/legal-updates/the-new-eu-general-product-safety-regulation-what-you-need-to-know)).
- **Packaging (NL):** below 50,000 kg/year no Verpact fee, but the calculation showing this must be kept on file (Known, [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/afvalbeheersbijdrage-verpakkingen-betalen/)).
- No CE directive applies to a passive storage tube; no textile labelling. *Believed.* REACH declaration for PVC/coating from tube supplier. *Believed – request with quote.*
