**V1 in one sentence:** three 1 m Martens Ecomar 75 mm tubes (the bore already proven at home) are each fitted with a glued socket and a stock screw-cap end stop, sprayed matt anthracite, strapped into a triangle and clipped onto a bamboo board. It tests whether the cluster stands, seals and photographs well enough for a Marktplaats listing.

**Critical part:** the Martens 75 mm *eindstop met schroefdeksel* (glues into a 75 mm socket). It gives the sealed, unscrewable bottom with no custom work. Its inner opening isn't listed anywhere I found, so whether a 66 mm can passes through it is **not known**.

## Build steps (tools: fine-tooth saw, file, drill/screwdriver, masking tape)
1. **In store first:** push a 330 ml can through the socket plus end stop with the cap off. If it doesn't pass, stop and look for a different end piece.
2. Deburr the pipe ends. Glue a socket onto the bottom of each tube, then glue the end stop into the socket. Cure as stated on the tin.
3. Key the surface lightly, mask the threads, prime, then two coats of anthracite. Paint the caps separately.
4. Stand the three tubes in a triangle and fit two straps at about 20% and 80% of the height.
5. Screw the two pipe brackets into the board at the right spacing and clip two of the tubes in just above the socket.
6. To empty: unclip, carry the cluster, then unscrew the caps at the Tomra machine.

## Known weaknesses vs the brief
- **Length:** 1 m tubes hold 9 cans, not 10 (**Known** from the home prototype). Ecomar is listed in 1 m only. The [Gamma RIO 2 m](https://www.gamma.nl/assortiment/martens-rio-buis-grijs-75-mm-2-meter/p/B301531) (€12.99) has a 3 mm wall, giving a ≈69 mm bore, below the 70.6–71 mm target. Too tight is **Believed**. Test one can in store before switching.
- **End-stop opening and the socket's stop ridge** may be narrower than 66 mm (**Guessed** risk, the opening isn't listed). This is the showstopper for "empties quickly".
- **Stability:** a strapped triangle only about 160 mm wide (**Believed**) still tips without the board and brackets. The board fixes this but makes the unit 45×28 cm.
- **Looks:** painted PVC with visible sockets and straps still reads as "nicely painted drainpipe" (**Believed**). It won't test the premium look; that's V2's job.
- **Paint durability:** spray paint on PVC chips at the thread and the bike-carry contact points (**Believed**).
