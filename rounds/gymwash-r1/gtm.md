# Go-to-Market – Gym-wear laundry brand – R1 (21 Nov 2026)

**Testability: Possible.** A paid first commitment (pre-order of a 3-month subscription, refundable) can be tested in 2 weeks for near-zero cost. True *repeat* payment cannot be observed in 2 weeks – it needs the second billing cycle (~6–8 weeks). [Believed]

**Beachhead.** HYROX/functional-fitness athletes in Amsterdam who wash synthetic kit several times a week. Dense, reachable and event-driven: HYROX Amsterdam 2024 had about 12,000 athletes at the RAI, with about 50,000 expected across Dutch and Belgian editions ([NH Nieuws](https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/341600/hyros-2024-rai-robert-van-herk)) [Known]. RAI has signed for 2026–2028, expanding to two weeks from 2027 ([RAI](https://www.rai.nl/en/news/hyrox-is-coming-to-rai-amsterdam-in-2026-2027-2028)) [Known]. Buyer = user = budget holder; self-serve.

**Where they are reached (NL).**
- HYROX partner gyms: TrainMore Amsterdam Oost and Sloterdijk; also Sin City Boxing and The Workout Lab (De Pijp) run HYROX sessions ([AmsterdamNow](https://www.amsterdamnow.com/en/lifestyle/hyrox-amsterdam-training-locations/)) [Known].
- HYROX Benelux event partnerships. Personal care already sits there (Rexona as national partner) ([HYROX Benelux](https://hyroxbenelux.com/eventpartners-hyrox-benelux-eng)); Original Source runs "shower experiences" at HYROX London ([Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/original-source-named-partner-for-hyrox-london-olympia)) [Known]. No laundry partner listed – a category gap, not proof of demand [Believed].
- Basic-Fit: 4.82m memberships, 1,660 clubs at end 2025; no in-club retail mentioned ([finanznachrichten](https://www.finanznachrichten.de/nachrichten-2026-01/67521023-basic-fit-n-v-strong-membership-trends-continued-in-q4-2025-early-2026-399.htm)) [Known]. Large, but a corporate sale – not the first ten.
- Creators, CrossFit boxes, running clubs: not researched by name this round.

**How the apparel brands talk about care.** Gymshark: cold, gentle cycle, vinegar pre-soak, sport-specific detergent if you have one, no fabric softener ("can coat fibers and trap unwanted smells") ([Gymshark](https://uk.gymshark.com/blog/article/how-to-remove-sweaty-smells-from-your-workout-clothes)). Lululemon: cold, hang dry, no softener; odour not addressed, no detergent sold or named ([lululemon](https://shop.lululemon.com/help/products-and-sizing/wash-and-care)) [Known]. Neither sells laundry products – a co-brand pitch is possible but not tested [Believed].

**Competition at the shelf (NL).** HG "Tegen nare geur in sportkleding" additive: €7.49 / 500 g / ~16 washes at Albert Heijn, claims "100% of odour molecules" removed ([AH](https://www.ah.nl/producten/product/wi203702/hg-tegen-nare-geur-in-sportkleding)) [Known] – i.e. ~€0.47 per wash, from those figures. Perwoll Renew Sport is also sold in NL ([Galaxus](https://www.galaxus.nl/en/s2/product/perwoll-renew-liquid-sport-27-washing-cycles-gel-liquid-laundry-detergent-fabric-care-43306210)). In the US, HEX Performance is in Target ([Target](https://www.target.com/p/hex-performance-fragrance-free-laundry-detergent-50oz/-/A-84591054)) [Known]. The message must beat "already on the AH shelf".

**Subscription DTC playbook.** smol (UK): 25p per wash, trial of 9 capsules for £1 postage, pause or change deliveries any time; Ariel pods up to 55p per wash, Aldi 10p ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/smol-and-other-laundry-detergent-subscriptions-are-they-worth-trying-aabyr4V4CZCU)) [Known]. Lesson: a low-cost trial leads straight into a pausable subscription [Believed].

**Pricing hypothesis (structure only).** A 3-month subscription box (two steps, dosed per wash) priced per wash against the HG (~€0.47) and Ariel-pod (up to 55p) anchors; a low-cost trial pack that leads into the subscription; pause any time. First price: [Ross: confirm]. B2B: a monthly bulk refill for gyms or towel services, priced per kg or per wash – [Ross: confirm]. Revenue model: **recurring** (subscription).

**Demand test (2 weeks).** Landing page with a refundable paid pre-order of a 3-month subscription. Traffic only from free channels: posts in HYROX/run-club communities, 50 DMs to athletes, a flyer at the front desk of 2 TrainMore clubs if allowed. Ship date stated honestly.
- **Pass:** ≥30 paid pre-orders, or ≥5% of unique visitors pay [Guessed threshold]; plus ≥2 gyms agree to stock or sample in showers.
- **Kill:** <10 pre-orders after ≥300 visitors, or most replies say "HG/vinegar already works for me".
- Cost: domain and payment page only – [Ross: confirm].
- Run in parallel with the CSO efficacy check: a pre-order on a product that doesn't beat HG is a refund liability.
