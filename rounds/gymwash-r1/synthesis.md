# CEO synthesis – Gym-wear laundry brand – R1 (21 Nov 2026)

**One-liner:** A gym-culture laundry brand for synthetic kit that keeps smelling after washing – most likely as a periodic "kit reset" plus a maintenance wash on subscription.

**Outcome type:** Side business first (white-label + Shopify subscription, ~4–8 h/week once running – Guessed). Scalable company only if efficacy is provably better *and* a cheap channel (HYROX/gym partnerships) holds CAC down.
**Revenue model:** recurring (subscription), with retail/Bol/gym wholesale alongside.
**Testability: Easy for efficacy, Possible for demand.** Efficacy: a home wash test in 2 weeks for <€30. Demand: a paid pre-order in 2 weeks; true repeat payment only at the second billing cycle (6–8 weeks).

## What the seats found
- **Science (CSO):** the smell is real and well explained. Polyester binds sebum and bacteria; biofilm survives cold washes; washing machines reseed odour bacteria (*Moraxella*) – strong, replicated evidence. **Ross's hypothesis (pre-wash bleach destroys capsule enzymes) is mostly wrong:** oxidation-resistant enzymes have been standard since 1985. The likelier cause: **liquid capsules contain no bleach**, cold washes don't kill bacteria, and the machine itself is a source. CTO adds that peracid does knock enzymes out when mixed, so sequencing is still sensible – but formulators already manage it.
- **Competition (Strategy, Wedge):** crowded. Persil Activewear (US, 2024), Perwoll Renew Sport, AH Sport, HG sport additive (€7.49/~16 washes at AH), Dr. Beckmann Sport, Halo (UK), HEX and WIN (US). Only sequenced system found: Miele TwinDos, locked to Miele machines. **No gym-culture brand in NL/EU found.** No consumer "reset" kit for already-saturated kit found.
- **Money (CFO):** US sports detergent carries a ~3x premium per load; UK/EU incumbents sell odour lines at mainstream prices (Persil Odour Defy ≈29p/wash). Contract MOQs 5,000–15,000 units per SKU – two SKUs doubles this. Laundry DTC is hard: Smol £44.7m revenue, still loss-making, growth slowed to +2.6%; Blueland spent >$1m/month on ads.
- **Build (CTO):** two-product kit from a small-MOQ private-label maker is the route (stock bio liquid with a Pristine-type enzyme + percarbonate/TAED powder; prewash then main wash). Booster likely needs CLP label + UFI. No IP – the routine can be copied.
- **Channel (GTM):** HYROX is the densest NL channel (~12,000 athletes at the RAI 2024, RAI signed to 2028, no laundry partner listed). TrainMore runs HYROX training. Gymshark and Lululemon sell no laundry products.

## Why the gap exists
- **WHY INCUMBENTS AREN'T DOING THIS:** they *are* selling single-bottle sport variants. A multi-step routine runs against 14 years of unit-dose convenience strategy (one SKU, one shelf slot). Liquids and pods ship without oxygen bleach because it destabilises them.
- **WHERE THE GAP APPEARS TO BE:** stripping odour that has already built up in polyester (it plateaus after 5–10 washes), plus a European brand that talks to gym culture. Both Guessed/Believed – not tested.
- **RISKS TO THE GAP:** the build-up may not be reversible with existing chemistry (showstopper); an oxygen-bleach bio powder at 40 °C may already fix it (then it's education, not product); Persil can add a "reset" SKU in one cycle.

## Showstopper
Does any off-the-shelf sequence remove built-up smell from polyester clearly better than a sport detergent or a plain bio powder at 40 °C? If not – kill.

## Minimum test (2 weeks, <€30, ~1 h/week)
Ross washes the same smelly kit four ways, 3–4 washes each, someone else scores blind after 24 h damp storage:
1. Current routine (capsule, Vanish).
2. Bio powder with oxygen bleach/TAED at 40 °C, after a hot empty machine-clean wash.
3. As (2) plus 30-min enzyme pre-soak.
4. A sport detergent from the shelf (AH Sport or HG additive).
**Pass:** (3) clearly beats (2) and (4) → there is a reset product to brand. **Kill/park:** (2) or (4) fixes it → gap is education; no brand.

Parallel, one conversation: Chris Callewaert (Ghent, textile malodour – tests detergents with industry, 2.5 h away) on whether anything beats a bio powder on "permastink".

## Optimist's case
It's Ross's own problem, testable at home this week for the cost of three bottles. If a reset routine wins, the brand, HYROX channel and subscription are all there – a low-cost side business with a clear audience.

## NABC
- **NEED:** Synthetic gym kit keeps smelling after washing; polyester holds sebum and bacteria, and odour plateaus after 5–10 washes. Gym-goers wash kit several times a week.
- **APPROACH:** A two-step "kit reset" (enzyme then activated oxygen) plus a maintenance wash, white-labelled, sold by subscription through HYROX and gym channels.
- **BENEFITS:** Kit that stops smelling instead of masked with fragrance; €/wash comparable to HG (~€0.47) [Ross: confirm price].
- **COMPETITION:** Persil/Perwoll sport, AH Sport, HG additive, HEX, WIN (US). None sells a reset system or a gym-culture brand in NL.
