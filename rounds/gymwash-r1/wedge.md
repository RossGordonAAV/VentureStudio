# Wedge check – Gym-wear laundry brand – R1 (21 Nov 2026)

**Wedge verdict: Wedge too close to incumbents** (and a crowded niche). "Sport detergent for synthetics" is already a shelf variant at the big players and is held by several niche brands.
- Henkel: Persil Activewear Clean (US, 2024, "eliminates odors") – Known ([Henkel](https://www.henkel-northamerica.com/press/press-releases-and-kits/2025-10-02-persil-laundry-detergent-announces-persil-activewear-clean-as-a-2025-product-of-the-year-usa-winner-2033748)). Perwoll Renew Sport in EU, claims it neutralises sweat odour on polyester/polyamide – Known ([Galaxus NL](https://www.galaxus.nl/en/s2/product/perwoll-renew-liquid-sport-27-washing-cycles-gel-liquid-laundry-detergent-fabric-care-43306210)).
- Niche: HEX raised $5m in 2020, 2,000+ US stores – Known ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/hex-performance-closes-5-million-funding-round-led-by-lykos-capital-partners-301023342.html)). WIN since 2006, Olympic-licensed – Known ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WIN_(detergent))), and already writes Gymshark-specific washing guides – Known ([WIN](https://www.windetergent.com/blogs/how-to/how-wash-gymshark-leggings)). UK: Halo ProActive at Sainsbury's, £8/25 washes, antibacterial – Known ([Sainsbury's](https://www.sainsburys.co.uk/groceries/product/halo-sports-laundry-liquid-1l-25-washes)); Grangers Active Wash in outdoor retail – Known ([Grangers](https://grangers.co.uk/products/active-wash)).

**Unbundled/sequenced system:** Believed to be marketing unless it measurably beats a sport detergent. Odour compounds build up in polyester and plateau after 5–10 washes whatever the detergent – Known ([UAlberta](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/folio/2020/07/research-reveals-why-its-hard-to-get-the-smell-out-of-polyester.html)). If sequencing does work, it can be done with off-the-shelf products (soak, then wash), so it is a know-how rather than a product. Contract manufacturers can copy it in months. No IP – Believed.

**The wedge, narrowed:** a periodic "kit reset" (deep strip of already-saturated polyester kit), plus a maintenance wash on subscription, sold to UK/NL gym-goers whose kit still smells after sport detergents – Guessed. Not found as a branded two-step system in the searches run.

**What winning gives:** a DTC subscriber list and community. Brand only, with no data or IP leverage – Believed. Subscription laundry can scale: Smol reached £44.7m revenue but was still loss-making in FY25 – Known ([The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/smol-invests-for-growth-at-expense-of-rising-losses/717807.article)). The CAC risk is real.

**Expansion path:**
- Wedge → ring 2: all athleisure, plus sports clubs' team-kit washing (B2B, recurring) – Believed.
- Ring 3: gym towels/commercial laundry (held by institutional chemical suppliers – Guessed), outdoor technical fabrics (Grangers is already there), odour sprays (HEX already sells one – [Trend Hunter](https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/hex-deodorizing-spray)), other EU countries. No sized market found.

**Two ways to win:** scalable company is weak: no moat, and it is a transactional/subscription hybrid. A side business is plausible: white label plus a Shopify subscription. Set-up cost, hours per week and margin per unit: [Ross: confirm].

## Why the gap exists
- **WHY INCUMBENTS AREN'T DOING THIS:** they *are* doing the single-bottle sport variant. A multi-step routine runs against their single-dose, convenience economics (one SKU, one shelf slot) – Believed.
- **WHERE THE GAP APPEARS TO BE:** removing odour that has *already built up* in polyester, which washing alone doesn't do ([UAlberta](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/folio/2020/07/research-reveals-why-its-hard-to-get-the-smell-out-of-polyester.html)). Also, no European brand with gym-culture branding was found – Guessed.
- **RISKS TO THE GAP:** the build-up may not be reversible with existing chemistry (showstopper). Persil/Perwoll can add a "reset" SKU. WIN/HEX could enter Europe. The fix may be a practice change, not a product.

**Question to answer next:** does any existing-chemistry sequence remove built-up odour from polyester better than a sport detergent? **Cheapest test (this week):** a blind sniff test on Ross's own kit and 5–10 gym-goers' kit. Compare Perwoll Sport/Halo with an off-the-shelf oxygen-bleach soak followed by an enzyme wash. If the off-the-shelf sequence wins, there is a reset product to brand. If nothing wins, kill.
