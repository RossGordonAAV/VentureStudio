# Audit – Gym-wear laundry brand – R1 (21 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: Fund with changes.** The efficacy test is cheap and worth running. It does not test the claim the business rests on, though.

**Outcome type and testability.** "Side business first" is fair. It is undermined by the 5,000–15,000-unit MOQ per SKU: set-up cost is not stated anywhere [Ross: confirm], and 4–8 h/week is Guessed. Calling efficacy "Easy" is fair. Calling demand "Possible" is honest, but no demand test is designed, only mentioned.

**Weakest points**
1. **Even a pass may not leave a product.** If arm 3 (bio powder + TAED + pre-soak) wins, it is built from shelf products and a routine. That is still "education, not product", the outcome the synthesis itself calls a kill. No IP, and Persil can copy it in one cycle.
2. **The test is too small to settle anything.** One person, one kit, 3–4 washes, a nose score from one rater. A fail would be easy to explain away and a pass easy to over-read. The 5–10-wash plateau also means "already-saturated" kit needs defining before starting.
3. **Price and channel don't add up.** The benefit says €/wash comparable to HG (~€0.47). The CFO says the margin case needs a ~3x premium and DTC laundry burns cash (Smol, Blueland). HYROX as a cheap channel is Believed: no partner talk, no CAC.

**Alternative actions (tick instead of or alongside)**
- [ ] **Call Callewaert before washing anything** (1 h). Ask whether anything off the shelf beats bio powder at 40 °C on permastink. *Kill* if he says no, or if a hot machine-clean plus bio powder is the known answer.
- [ ] **Widen the efficacy test**: same four arms, run by 5–8 HYROX/TrainMore athletes on their own kit, with blind scoring by a second person. *Pass:* arm 3 preferred by ≥6 of 8. *Kill:* arm 2 or 4 ties.
- [ ] **Fake-door demand test now, in parallel**: a landing page for the "Kit Reset" at a stated price [Ross: confirm price], with paid pre-orders pushed through HYROX community groups. Ad budget [Ross: confirm]. *Pass:* a pre-order threshold Ross sets in advance. *Kill:* clicks with no payments. An alternative wedge to test on the same page: a content/affiliate "fix your permastink" guide, which needs no stock and no MOQ.

**Opportunity cost:** the first two weeks are cheap. Holding them requires Ross to commit now to a kill if arm 2 or arm 4 wins. (Minor: the people list jumps from 4 to 10.)
