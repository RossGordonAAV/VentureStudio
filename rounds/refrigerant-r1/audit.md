# Audit – Legacy refrigerant destruction – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: Fund with changes.**

## Weakest points
1. **The case hinges on a Believed claim.** EU-origin CFC creditability is marked Believed, and the EU regulation text has not been read. Without credits, the only positive margin (R-12) disappears.
2. **The pass/kill criteria are lopsided.** Pass is an OR (one developer or 3 of 10 installers), while kill needs both to fail. "Would hand it over" is not willingness to pay, and HCFC is counted although it is shown not to pay. A fail would almost never be recorded.
3. **Two businesses are tested in one go, mostly through interested parties.** Tradewater and A-Gas are asked where they won't compete. Supply is untested: how much CFC is still held in NL, decades after the ban, is not stated. The timing is also inconsistent: "one week" in the decision, 14 days in the test.

## Alternative actions
1. **Credit gate first (desk, then one call).** Read the EU ODS recovery obligation and the VM0016 v2.0 surplus clause (about 2 hours). This is desk-based because the text exists and nobody needs to be asked about it. It also makes the call sharper. Then one call with a VM0016 developer or validator who is not a competitor. *Pass:* a named EU-origin category (e.g. abandoned stockpiles) with a stated verification route and cost per batch. *Kill:* the surplus test fails for every EU-origin category. If so, drop the credit version.
2. **Pivot A as a price test (calls).** Phone the same 10 installers, but quote a pickup price per kg ([Ross: confirm price]) and ask what they pay today. *Pass:* at least 3 accept the quoted price and name real volumes. *Kill:* at least 6 already get free wholesaler take-back or would pay nothing.
3. **Supply check before Pivot B (calls).** Ask 2–3 wholesalers how many kg of CFC still come back each year and in what lot sizes. *Pass:* volumes reach the minimum verification lot within a year. *Kill:* only scattered sub-kg lots. Pivot B can be parked until then, as it is Tradewater's home turf.
