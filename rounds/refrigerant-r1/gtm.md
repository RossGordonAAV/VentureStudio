# Go-to-Market – Legacy refrigerant destruction – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Beachhead.** Supply: NL refrigeration installers certified for F-gas work (BRL 100/200 via STEK, Kiwa or Bureau Veritas) who already hold cylinders of recovered R-12/R-22. They pool many small holders, so one installer replaces dozens of farms, sheds and garages. Buyer: their owner or director. Demand: corporate buyers of "super-pollutant" credits. **Believed**.

**What we know about the two sides**
- Installers must record how much refrigerant they recover and who they hand it to (CE Delft 2026, p. 38). Some reuse recovered gas without treating it; ILT enforces against this. **Known** ([CE Delft](https://ce.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CE_Delft_240505_Naleving_van_de_F-gassenverordening_Def.pdf))
- What NL installers pay for, or are paid for, recovered gas: **not found**. NVKL guidance covers recovery and reclaim but not disposal routes or costs ([NVKL](https://www.nvkl.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/NVKL-digitaal-informatiepakket-voor-klanten-van-leden.pdf)). One benchmark from elsewhere: New Zealand's Cool-Safe scheme pays holders NZ$40/kg ([Cooling Post](https://www.coolingpost.com/world-news/refrigerant-buy-back-scheme-raises-payments/)). **Known**
- Incumbent: A-Gas already runs "Rapid Recovery" in NL, e.g. 19.5 t recovered with Equans in Limburg ([A-Gas](https://agas.com/uk/news-insights/a-recovery-operation-in-the-netherlands-for-almost-20-tons-of-refrigerant-gas)). **Known**. It could be a partner or a competitor.
- EU-sourced credits exist. France accounts for 4.8% of ODS credits issued ([RMI](https://rmi.org/technical-explainer-ozone-depleting-substances-carbon-credits/)). Verra VM0016 v2.0 (Dec 2025) covers ODS and HFCs in all Montreal Protocol countries ([Verra](https://verra.org/verra-releases-revised-methodology-for-the-destruction-of-ozone-depleting-substances-and-hydrofluorocarbons/)). **Known**. Additionality under EU rules is not covered in either source.
- Buyers and prices. Google has signed long-term super-pollutant credit deals with Recoolit (refrigerants) and Cool Effect; prices were not disclosed ([ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/?p=20329)). Brown University bought 137,500 Tradewater credits ([Giving Green](https://www.givinggreen.earth/research/tradewater)). Published prices: Tradewater about $18/t (2023) ([Giving Green](https://www.givinggreen.earth/research/tradewater)), Recoolit pilot about $75/t (2022) ([Giving Green](https://www.givinggreen.earth/research/refrigerants)). Credits with the ICVCM CCP label sell at up to about 25% more ([BusinessGreen](https://www.businessgreen.com/news/4522833/study-core-carbon-principle-labelled-credits-command-cent-price-premium)). **Known**
- Destruction route already in use: Tradewater ships gas to Remondis in Bramsche, Germany ([Cool Effect](https://www.cooleffect.org/project/gas-buster)). **Known**
