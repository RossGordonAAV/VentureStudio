# CEO synthesis – Legacy refrigerant destruction – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

## We believe
- **Destruction is a solved problem; the AEB route is not.** AEB's acceptance terms exclude pressure vessels and cap fluorine compounds at 0.1%. Under the Montreal Protocol, municipal incineration is approved only for dilute sources such as foams. EU law allows only approved technologies. Licensed routes exist: A-Gas (NL, own plasma arc), Indaver (Antwerp), Chemours (Dordrecht) and Remondis (Bramsche, DE). (Known)
- **Per kg, only CFCs pay.** R-12 is worth about $128–184 per kg gross in credits at the two prices found ($12.50–18/t). The full chain of collection, transport and destruction costs $18–48 per kg. R-22 and R-134a at kg scale don't cover that, which confirms Ross's point on lower GWPs. (Known inputs; margin Believed)
- **EU-sourced gas probably earns no credits.** EU law already requires ODS to be recovered from equipment, so it fails the "beyond what the law requires" test. ACR covers only CFCs. CAR covers only Article 5 countries. Verra's VM0016 v2.0 (Dec 2025) applies everywhere but tests the same surplus. (Believed – full EU regulation text not read)
- **Incumbents already own the credit model.** Tradewater is backed by private equity and does small-lot, credit-funded collection outside the EU. A-Gas runs Rapid Recovery in NL and has its own destruction and credits. Climalife reclaims lots of 300 kg and up. (Known)
- **There is a real Dutch gap, but it's a collection and compliance gap.** More than 30% of F-gases leak. A take-back obligation (afgifteplicht) is announced for 2026, and EU rules add EPR by 2028 and mobile-equipment recovery from Mar 2027. The long tail of farms, garages and SMEs is poorly served. (Known / Believed)

## We don't know
- Whether any EU-origin gas can be credited. Abandoned stockpiles are the most likely exception.
- What NL holders pay today to dispose of old gas, if anything.
- Verification cost per batch and minimum lot. Each container needs a ≥0.45 kg lab sample, which forces consolidation.
- A current credit price; only two trades were found.

## Proposed decision: Keep going – one week to settle the credit question
The credit version stands or falls on EU eligibility, and that's a phone call, not research. If it fails there are two pivots, to be tested in the same calls:
- **Pivot A:** a paid kg-scale collection service for Dutch holders under the afgifteplicht.
- **Pivot B:** an EU-based aggregator or broker for non-EU CFC stocks. This is Tradewater's home turf.

Wedge verdict: **too close to incumbents** as briefed. Pivot A may be an open niche below A-Gas's and Climalife's lot sizes.
