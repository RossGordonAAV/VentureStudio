# Wedge check – Legacy refrigerant destruction – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Wedge verdict: Wedge too close to incumbents (and, in the EU, closed by regulation for the credit model).**

**The wedge as briefed:** kg-scale CFC/HCFC/HFC lots from EU garages, cold stores, SMEs and installers, destroyed for carbon credits.

**Why it isn't an open gap**
- Small lots are already Tradewater's model: gas is collected from garages, older HVAC firms, seizures and importers' unsold stock, then consolidated (US warehouse near Chicago; projects in about 10 Global South/Gulf countries). Its stated logic is that credits pay *because no end-of-life collection rule exists* there. **Known** ([Climate Confident, Tim Brown](https://www.buzzsprout.com/1329991/12059421))
- A-Gas already sells recovery, reclamation and destruction, with credits under Verra, CAR, ACR and CORSIA. **Known** ([A-Gas](https://www.agas.com/sustainability/carbon-credits/))
- EU law already requires ODS recovery at end of life, prevention of ODS foam emissions at demolition, and reclaim (not destruction) of halons. **Known** ([European Commission](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/ozone-layer/ods-legislation_en)) The EU ETS also bans HFC-destruction allowances. **Known** ([EIA 2026](https://eia-international.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-EIA-Evaluating-Options-for-Sustainably-Financed-Action-on-ODS-and-HFC-Banks.pdf)) Verra VM0016 v2.0 (Dec 2025) applies to non-Article 5 countries and now covers HFCs, foams and fire suppressants. **Known** ([Verra](https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0016-destruction-of-ozone-depleting-substances-and-hydrofluorocarbons-v2-0/)) EU-sourced gas is still likely to fail the regulatory-surplus test. **Believed** (VCS surplus rule not checked in full text)
- NL: Renewi E-waste (ex-Coolrec) and HKS already process refrigerants. Leakage is estimated at over 30%, and a take-back obligation (afgifteplicht) was announced for 2026. **Known** ([CE Delft, Jan 2026](https://cedelft.eu/publications/dutch-compliance-with-the-fluorinated-greenhouse-gases-regulation/))
- The leakage points to a real gap in the EU, but it is a compliance and collection gap, not a credit gap. **Believed**
- AEB burning canisters: not checked. Not found as an approved ODS destruction route. **Guessed** that it is unlikely to be permitted.

**What winning would give:** holder relationships with garages and installers, and data on where the gas is held. In the EU the verification capability matters less, because credits are doubtful. **Believed**

**Expansion path (if pivoted)**
- **Wedge → NL "afgifte" collection service:** paid kg-scale pick-up and paperwork for holders under the new take-back obligation, possibly with a deposit or return premium (both options were discussed by CE Delft). **Believed**
- **Ring 2: other EU countries and F-gas compliance services,** reusing the same holder network. **Guessed**
- **Ring 3: credit-funded collection in Article 5 countries,** where banks rose from 3,543 kt (2014) to 4,700 kt (2022). **Known** ([EIA 2026](https://eia-international.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-EIA-Evaluating-Options-for-Sustainably-Financed-Action-on-ODS-and-HFC-Banks.pdf)) This is Tradewater's home turf. Foams (now in VM0016) are a possible ring 3b. EU halons are a dead end, because the law requires reclaim. **Known**/**Guessed**
- No market sizes in € were found.

**Question to answer next:** Can EU-sourced gas earn any voluntary credit at all? If not, would holders pay for collection?

**Cheapest test:** one call with Tradewater on EU eligibility. One call with CE Delft on the afgifteplicht and how holders will pay.
