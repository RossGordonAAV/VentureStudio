# CTO – Legacy refrigerant destruction – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

**Core technical risk:** Destruction itself is solved; the hard bit is producing credit-grade proof per container at kg scale. VM0016 v2.0 requires each container to be weighed full and empty on one calibrated scale at the destruction plant (within 2 days before and after destruction), and a ≥0.45 kg liquid sample from **each** container, taken by an unaffiliated technician and analysed (AHRI 700/ISO) by an accredited, unaffiliated lab. Containers that fail get no credits. **Known** ([VM0016 v2.0 PDF](https://verra.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/VM0016-Destruction-of-Ozone-Depleting-Substances-and-Hydrofluorocarbons-v2.0.pdf), §9.3.1). A 0.45 kg sample from a few-kg cylinder means small cylinders must be consolidated first, which triggers the mixed-gas rules (third-party circulation in a tank with liquid and vapour ports, <90% single species = "mixed"). **Known**, same source.

**AEB is not a route.** AEB's acceptance terms ban pressure vessels ("drukhouders") and cap fluorine compounds at 0.1% and Cl/Br/I organics at 1% **Known** ([AEB acceptatievoorwaarden](https://www.aebamsterdam.nl/media/052p4dxl/2022-12-20_aeb-exploitatie-bv-acceptatievoorwaarden-ned.pdf)). Gas cylinders explode in AEB's ovens, so AEB now shreds nitrous-oxide cylinders before burning them **Known** ([NH Nieuws, Mar 2025](https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/346301/nieuwe-lachgasloods-moet-explosies-in-ovens-aeb-voorkomen-heel-veel-schade-voor-bedrijf)). Under Montreal Protocol decisions, municipal waste incineration is approved only for *dilute* sources such as foams **Known** ([Dec. XXX/6](https://www.informea.org/en/decision/decision-xxx6-destruction-technologies-controlled-substances); [Dec. XXIII/12](https://ozone.unep.org/node/1662)). EU law allows ODS to be destroyed only by technology approved by the Parties **Known** ([Reg. (EU) 2024/590, Art. 20(6)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/590/oj)). The AEB test should be dropped.

**Approved technologies for concentrated gas** include rotary kiln, liquid-injection incineration, argon plasma arc, cement kilns (not halons) and others **Known** (Dec. XXIII/12, XXX/6). **EU plants:** the EU's June 2026 list to UNEP puts Indaver NV (Antwerp) and Chemours Netherlands (Dordrecht) in what looks like the destruction block. A-Gas Nederland (Eygelshoven) and Eco-Collect (Bergen op Zoom) are listed with the recovery/reclamation firms **Known** (list) / **Believed** (reading of the block) ([EU list](https://ozone.unep.org/sites/default/files/additional-reported-information/decisionsXXXVII3/EU.pdf)). A-Gas runs its own PyroPlas plasma arc and links destruction to Verra/CAR/ACR credits **Known** ([A-Gas](https://agas.com/products-and-services/destruction-and-disposal-services)). Where its plant is located: not found. ATM Moerdijk: no evidence found that it takes refrigerants. Renewi's cylinder terms exclude "koelgassen" **Known** ([Renewi](https://renewi.com/-/media/renewi-old/acceptatievoorwaarden/be-nl-accvwdn/2025_02/nlrenewi-acceptatievoorwaardengasflessen-niet-brandbaar652019.pdf)).

**Gate that decides the route (flag for CEO/CFO):** EU Art. 20(1) requires ODS in equipment to be recovered for destruction, recycling or reclamation **Known** (Reg. 2024/590). VMD0048 requires regulatory surplus and excludes gas from schemes that already drive destruction **Known** ([VMD0048 v2.0](https://verra.org/methodologies/vmd0048-activity-method-for-the-determination-of-additionality-for-recovered-and-stockpiled-ods-refrigerants-v2-0)). The main credit gap is therefore EU-recovered CFC/HCFC **Believed**. Stockpiles and abandoned small holdings may be the eligible niche **Guessed**.
