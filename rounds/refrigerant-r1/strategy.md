## Rules that shape the field
- **ACR International ODS** covers **CFCs only** and credits only reductions "in excess" of what law requires (Known, [ACR v1.0](https://acrcarbon.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ACR-ODS-International-v1.0-2021-04.pdf)). EU Reg. 2024/590 requires recovery from equipment and bans the use of ODS (Known, [EC](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/ozone-layer/ods-legislation_en), [EPRS](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/RegData/etudes/BRIE/2022/738195/EPRS_BRI(2022)738195_EN.pdf)). EU CFCs are therefore likely not surplus (Believed).
- **Verra VM0016 v2.0** (15 Dec 2025) is the first to credit HFC destruction and HCFC stockpiles, using the VT0008 additionality test (Known, [Verra](https://verra.org/verra-releases-revised-methodology-for-the-destruction-of-ozone-depleting-substances-and-hydrofluorocarbons/)). EU F-gas Reg. 2024/573 already obliges operators to have gas recovered and then recycled, reclaimed or destroyed (Known, [EIA](https://eia-international.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-EIA-A-Concise-Guide-to-the-EU-F-Gas-Regulation-SINGLE-PAGES.pdf)). The EU baseline is likely reclaim, not venting, so additionality for EU HFCs is weak (Believed).
- Issuance to date: about 7 m credits, 74.6% of them US. "France 4.8%" probably reflects where the gas was destroyed, not where it came from (Known / Believed, [RMI](https://rmi.org/technical-explainer-ozone-depleting-substances-carbon-credits/); [Carbon Pulse](https://carbon-pulse.com/256372/): Chilean gas sent to France).
- Only one price was found: $12.50/t for 9,000 ODS credits in Apr 2025 (Known, [QCI](https://qcintel.com/carbon/article/us-university-pays-12-50-t-for-9-000-ods-carbon-credits-38920.html)). Unit economics belong to the CFO seat.
- **AEB test idea:** AEB's acceptance terms exclude "drukhouders en tanks" (Known, [AEB](https://www.aebamsterdam.nl/media/052p4dxl/2022-12-20_aeb-exploitatie-bv-acceptatievoorwaarden-ned.pdf)). Only destruction technologies approved under the Montreal Protocol may be used (Known, [EIA](https://eia-international.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-EIA-A-Concise-Guide-to-the-EU-F-Gas-Regulation-SINGLE-PAGES.pdf)). The canister test is a no.

## Why we win / why we lose
- **Win:** the long tail of farms, garages and SMEs that A-Gas and Climalife's ≥300 kg services don't serve (Believed). NL leakage gaps are documented (Known, CE Delft). Gas could be aggregated for an existing developer rather than run as our own credit project (Believed).
- **Lose:** the EU additionality test fails. Tradewater and A-Gas already hold the methodologies, buyers and destroyers. Cylinders below 1 kg cost more in logistics and chain-of-custody checks than they earn (Guessed).

## Why now
VM0016 v2.0 (Dec 2025) opens HFCs and HCFC stockpiles. EPR schemes for F-gases in waste equipment must exist by 2028, and recovery from mobile equipment starts 12 Mar 2027 (Known, Verra; EIA).

## Pre-mortem (CB Insights)
1. **Need/lack business model (primary):** EU gas earns no credits.
2. **Pricing/cost issues:** kg-scale collection costs exceed the value of the credits.
3. **Get outcompeted:** Tradewater (PE-backed) or A-Gas adds a small-holder pick-up service.

Failed predecessor: EOS Climate. California planned to invalidate 231,154 of its 2012 ODS credits because the destruction plant (Clean Harbors) may have breached its permit (Known, [EEL](https://www.environmentenergyleader.com/articles/33063-california-to-invalidate-carbon-credits)). EOS's current status: not found.

## Strategic test
One 20-minute call with Tradewater this week. Question: "Would you credit EU-sourced CFC-12 or R-22 under ACR or VM0016 v2.0? If not, which non-EU sources do you lack collectors for?" A "no" kills the EU version and points to a pivot: an EU-based aggregator or broker for non-EU stockpiles.
