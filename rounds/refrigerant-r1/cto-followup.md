# CTO follow-up – Ross's three challenges – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

## 1. Meter the gas in, don't burn the cylinder
**Technically plausible, legally blocked.**
- **Furnace conditions.** The EU incineration rule (IED Art. 50(2)) requires 850 °C for 2 s, rising to 1,100 °C where hazardous waste holds >1% halogenated organics. **Known** ([IED Art. 50](https://legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2010/75/article/50/adopted/data.htm?view=plain)) AEB's 2013 permit caps organic chlorine in hazardous waste at 1%. **Known** ([AEB permit](https://www.aebamsterdam.nl/media/gttdtcp0/definitieve-beschikking-revisievergunning-wabo-2013.pdf), §6.3.5) Its acceptance terms cap fluorine at 0.1%. **Known** (R1)
- **Acid gas.** R-12 is about 31% F and 59% Cl by mass, so each kg forms roughly 0.33 kg HF. **Believed** (stoichiometry) A slow, metered feed into a large line would probably stay inside the scrubber's capacity. A comparable rule: TEAP gives cement kilns "conservative limits" of 0.25% F in the total feed. **Known** ([TEAP 2018](https://www.cooleffect.org/lib/content/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/TEAP-DecXXIX4-TF-Report-April2018.pdf), p.26)
- **Performance bar.** Concentrated sources need ≥99.99% DRE, HF ≤5 mg/Nm³, HCl ≤100 mg/Nm³ and dioxins ≤0.2 ng ITEQ/Nm³. Dilute sources need 95% DRE. TEAP reported no destruction data for municipal incinerators (MSWI) and noted emissions above the ODS criteria. **Known** (TEAP 2018, pp.1-2, 12, 29)
- **Legal.** EU law allows only Parties-approved technologies. **Known** ([Reg. 2024/590 Art. 20(6)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/590/oj)) Decision XXX/6 approves MSWI only for *diluted* sources. Liquid injection, gaseous/fume oxidation, rotary kiln, reactor cracking, cement kiln, porous thermal reactor and plasma arc are approved for concentrated sources. **Known** ([Dec. XXX/6](https://www.informea.org/en/decision/decision-xxx6-destruction-technologies-controlled-substances)) Approval is per technology, not per feed method, so a lance in an MSWI is still an MSWI. **Believed** It would need DRE test data, a TEAP assessment, a decision by the Parties and a permit change, which means years. **Believed**
- **NL/BE/DE routes for concentrated gas:**
  - **Chemours Dordrecht:** the permit accepts EWC 14 06 01 (CFC/HCFC/HFC) up to 1,000 t/yr. A "thermal converter" has its own HF/Cl₂ emission rules. **Known** ([draft permit 2022](https://repository.officiele-overheidspublicaties.nl/Bijlagen/TerInzageLegging/2022/til-2022-1560/1/bijlage/753217_Ontwerpbeschikking.pdf)) Feed method: not found.
  - **Remondis SAVA, Brunsbüttel:** rotary kiln at 950–1,200 °C, then >1,100 °C for >2 s; HF measured at 0.1 against a 1.0 mg/m³ limit. **Known** ([SAVA](https://www.remondis-industrie-service.de/fileadmin/user_upload/ris_2020/download_neu/sonstige_downloads/240924_Broschuere-REMONDIS_SAVA_en.pdf)) Whether it takes gas cylinders: not stated.
  - **Indaver Antwerp:** feed method not found.
  - **A-Gas:** own plasma arc. **Known** (R1)
  - **Crealis, Bergen op Zoom:** its application says CFCs/HCFCs are destroyed, preferably converted to HCl and HF, but ILT found it contradictory. **Known** ([ILT 2021](https://www.ilent.nl/binaries/ilt/documenten/leefomgeving-en-wonen/overheidsinstanties-en-wabo/wabo-vergunningen-grote-bedrijven/zienswijzen/advies-revisievergunning-crealis-nederland-b.v/Advies+revisievergunning+Crealis+Nederland+B.V.++H536682.pdf))
- **Detour:** keep Ross's "meter it in" logic, but aim it at a plant that is already approved (Chemours Dordrecht, Indaver or SAVA).

## 2. What NL holders pay to get rid of leftover gas
**No published NL € price list found.** Prices are set by quote.
- **A-Gas EU terms (Mar 2026):**
  - cylinder rent per month after a free period, both set in the quote;
  - deposit can be forfeited;
  - destruction costs are charged when products no longer approved in the EU (i.e. CFC/HCFC) are returned;
  - transport fee for extra visits.

  No amounts are given. **Known** ([A-Gas T&Cs](https://www.agas.com/media/ejulilak/nl-a-gas-european-standard-terms-and-conditions-mar-2026.pdf))
- **A-Gas Rapid Exchange:** A-Gas pays for recovered contents by type and purity, at no cost apart from cylinder rent after the free loan. **Known** ([A-Gas](https://www.agas.com/nl/products-and-services/rapid-exchange/)) The page carries a non-NL phone number, so it may be US copy. **Believed**
- **Westfalen (DE):** customers can return used gas at any time; no prices published. **Known** ([Westfalen](https://westfalen.com/fileadmin/user_upload/Dateien_DE/PDF/GE/Westfalen_GE_Positionspapier_Kaeltemittel_Westfalen-AG.pdf))
- **Partly empty cylinders:** installers return them to avoid rent, and often leave 2–3 kg in them. **Known** ([AREA](https://area-eur.be/sites/default/files/2018-08/AREA%20Technical%20Bulletin%20Recycling%20Fgas%20%282018-07-NL%29.pdf))
- **Not found:** Climalife/Dehon, Eco-Collect and Linde prices.
- **Only per-kg figure found (not NL):** NZ Cool-Safe pays NZ$40 (≈€22) per kg for recovered gas. **Known** ([Cooling Post](https://www.coolingpost.com/world-news/refrigerant-buy-back-scheme-raises-payments/))
- **Reading:** recovered HFCs are paid for or taken free; CFC/HCFC is charged. **Believed** € per kg and minimum per cylinder: [Ross: confirm via quote].

## 3. How much CFC/HCFC still comes back
- **EU, 2025:** 6,224 t of Annex I substances were destroyed, of which 88% was carbon tetrachloride, 5% HCFC and 4% CFC by mass. **Known** ([EEA 2026](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/topics/in-depth/climate-change-mitigation-reducing-emissions/ozone-depleting-substances-2026)) That is about 310 t HCFC and 250 t CFC EU-wide, including non-refrigerant use. **Believed** (arithmetic) There is no split between recovered and virgin.
- **Germany:** 19 fridge-recycling plants recovered 147–156 t/yr of refrigerant in 2019–21, not split by type. **Known** ([LAGA](https://www.laga-online.de/documents/bericht-an-umk-zur-rueckgewinnung-von-kaeltemittel-2022-02-27_1683281751.pdf))
- **NL:** no CFC/HCFC return figures were found. The CE Delft 2026 compliance report gives none. **Known** ([CE Delft](https://ce.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CE_Delft_240505_Naleving_van_de_F-gassenverordening_Def.pdf)) Lot-size data: not found anywhere. Only wholesalers know this, so it is a phone question.

**Smallest test:** ask Chemours Dordrecht and A-Gas NL for a written quote to take back 1 × 5 kg R-12 and 1 × 10 kg R-22 cylinder (rent, per-kg fee, minimum charge). That gives the cost floor for Pivot A.
