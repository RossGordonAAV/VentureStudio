# CSO follow-up – Legacy refrigerant destruction – R1 (20 Nov 2026)

## 1. Credit gate (desk read)
**Short answer: yes, one category may pass. That is neat CFC/HCFC held in cylinders that are not part of any equipment** (orphan, shelf or abandoned stock). Every other EU source fails or is doubtful. (Believed; based on the texts below, with no VVB or developer view yet.)

**The bar.** NL is a high-income country, so an enforcement-gap argument is not available. VCS Standard v5.0 §3.13.1(1) says the activity must be "not mandated by any law or regulatory framework". [Verra](https://verra.org/documents/vcs-standard-v5-0/) (Known)

**The loophole.** The Reg. 2024/590 recovery duties cover only "products and equipment", and Art 2(13) defines that term as "…except containers, used for transporting or storing ozone-depleting substances". [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32024R0590) (Known)

| EU-origin source | Decisive clause (Reg. 2024/590 unless stated) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Gas in RAC equipment and heat pumps | Art 20(1): "be recovered for destruction, recycling or reclamation". Art 4(1) bans use, so destruction is the only lawful end | Fails in practice (Believed). VM0016 also rules projects ineligible once compliance exceeds 50% |
| Foams in buildings | Art 20(2)–(3): handling "in a way that ensures the destruction of those substances" | Fails (Known) |
| Halons | Art 20(4): "The destruction of halons shall be prohibited unless…" | Out of scope (Known) |
| Seized illegal imports | Art 17(11): customs seize them "for their disposal". Art 15(2) covers non-refillable containers: "disposal by destruction". VMD0048 allows only confiscated gas "that are not mandated for destruction" | Mostly fails. Volumes are probably tiny, because illegal trade is mainly HFC (Guessed) |
| **Neat stock in cylinders (orphan, shelf, abandoned)** | No Art 20 duty applies, because containers are excluded. Art 12 only *permits* sale "for destruction" or reclamation | **Candidate** (Believed) |

**Methodology fit for cylinder stock.**
- VMD0048 v2.0 lists "consumer quantity" cylinders of ≤250 lb (~113 kg) as a source category (positive list). [VMD0048](https://verra.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/VMD0048-Activity-Method-for-the-Determination-of-Additionality-for-Recovered-and-Stockpiled-ODS-Refrigerants-v2.0.pdf) (Known)
- VM0016 v2.0 accepts gas from "government or privately held stockpiles". [VM0016](https://verra.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/VM0016-Destruction-of-Ozone-Depleting-Substances-and-Hydrofluorocarbons-v2.0.pdf) (Known)
- ACR's legal test is about whether destruction itself is required: "If no law… requires the destruction of ODS stocks…". It covers CFCs only. [ACR](https://acrcarbon.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ACR-ODS-International-v1.0-2021-04.pdf) (Known text. That an EU source can use it is Believed.)

**Three catches that reduce the CFO's per-kg figure** (CFO to rerun; no number plugged here):
1. **Lower baseline.** In non-Article 5 countries VM0016 sets venting at 0%. Stockpiles count only through leakage: EF = 1 − (1 − LR)^10. The default LR of 10%/yr applies only to *government* stockpiles that cannot legally be sold, which gives about 65% of full GWP. Private stock needs a project-specific leak rate. (Known formula; the 65% is arithmetic)
2. **Exclusions.** VMD0048 excludes gas collected through "a product stewardship scheme". The 2026 NL afgifteplicht and the EU EPR due by 2028 could trigger this. (Believed)
3. **Dutch waste law.** If discarded ODS must be destroyed under national waste rules (e.g. a LAP3 minimum standard), cylinder stock fails too. Not found or read. (Guessed)
